Privacy policy

Real Reader collects no personal data

Last updated 19 November 2026. Applies to Real Reader (The Real Reader’s Companion) for iPhone, published by Kelsick Photography, LLC.

No personal data collected

Real Reader does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Downloading books

To show the list of available companions and to download the ones you choose, the app fetches plain files from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests carry no account, identifier, cookie or reading data; they only ask for the list or for a book.

Like any web host, GitHub receives the network information that every request includes, such as your IP address. We don’t receive or keep it. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Downloaded books are kept on your iPhone so they work offline.

Requesting a book

If you ask for a book to be added, the app sends the title and author you typed, and nothing else: no name, email address, account or device identifier. We use it only to decide which companions to write.

Requests are delivered to us by Formspree, a form service, which receives the network information that every request includes, such as your IP address. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your place, the app saves your quiz scores, answers in progress, your current chapter and the days you read. This is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

It is included in your normal iPhone backups, and deleting the app deletes it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
