Privacy policy

Real Reader collects nothing

Last updated 10 November 2026. Applies to Real Reader (The Real Reader’s Companion) for iPhone, published by Kelsick Photography, LLC.

No data collected

Real Reader does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your place, the app saves your quiz scores, answers in progress, your current chapter and the days you read. This is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

It is included in your normal iPhone backups, and deleting the app deletes it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
