Get in touch

Questions, problems, or ideas for books to add? Open an issue on GitHub and we’ll reply there.

Open an issue

Think a quiz answer is wrong?

Tell us the book, the chapter and the question, and we’ll check it against the text.

Your progress

  • Scores, quizzes in progress, your current chapter and your streak are saved on your iPhone.
  • They carry over when the app updates, and come back if you restore your iPhone from a backup.
  • Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account, so they can’t be recovered another way.
